PACKAGE CONTENTS

1. Radio_Z13_Research_Summary.pdf
   Research summary for reading and distribution.

2. Radio_Z13_Research_Summary.docx
   Editable copy of the same report.

3. Radio_Z13_Simulator_1_Circular_Receiver.html
   Historical Phase 1 state: A-Z ring, bezel, and 12:1 coupled hands.

4. Radio_Z13_Simulator_2_GFRPOSTE_Sector.html
   Historical Phase 2 state: thirteen-letter sector containing the subset
   compatible with G.F.R.POSTE. The file itself states that the selection
   of eight letters and their order have not yet been demonstrated.

5. Radio_Z13_Simulator_3_Instrument_v0_9.html
   Current frozen instrument: clock, bezel, 0°-360° protractor, and
   simultaneous 24- and 26-letter rings.

6. LEEME.docx
   Author's Note in Spanish.

7. README.docx
   Author's Note in English.

8. SHA256SUMS.txt
   SHA-256 hashes of the package files.

METHODOLOGICAL NOTICE

This package does not present a validated cryptographic solution to Z13.
It publishes a research path, observations, measurements, and tools so
that they can be tested, criticized, modified, or refuted.

In the documents, “the author” refers to the person who developed and
directed this research. “The sender of Z13” refers to whoever created or
sent the cryptogram; it is not assumed that this necessarily corresponds
to the same person behind all crimes attributed to Zodiac.
