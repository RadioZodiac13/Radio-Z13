Z13 · Simulator 2 · Historical G.F.R.POSTE Sector

Intermediate Phase 2 instrument: 60-position bezel, 12:1 coupled hands, 12-pair ring, and 24-letter outer ring.

What this copy shows: in the base position, the highlighted sector contains seven outer letters - I, J, K, L, N, O, P - and three pairs - E/R, F/S, G/T - for thirteen individual letters in total.

Within that set are the eight letters of G.F.R.POSTE. It is a historical candidate reading: an internal rule is still needed to select those eight letters and determine their order. The orange layer documents the observation; it does not add a cryptographic rule.

24-letter outer ring: clock positions 1–24 = A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I, J, K, L, N, O, P, Q, R, S, T, U, V, W, X, Y. On a real 24-hour dial, position 24 is at the top, 6 at the right, 12 at the bottom, and 18 at the left.
Fixed A-L sector ring: each hourly sector of the clock carries a fixed letter: 0–1=A, 1–2=B, 2–3=C, 3–4=D, 4–5=E, 5–6=F, 6–7=G, 7–8=H, 8–9=I, 9–10=J, 10–11=K, 11–12=L. Zodiac symbol: integrated into the 3 o’clock mark inside the clock so that it does not interfere with the outer reading.
